Admissions Process

Your Journey to SKDU

  • Step 1: Explore & Discover

    Explore our renowned programs in Martial Arts, Philosophy, and more. Discover our unique 'Mentorship of Masters' program and find the perfect path for you.

  • Step 2: Prepare Your Application

    Gather your transcripts, letters of recommendation, and write a personal essay on your journey towards inner peace and knowledge. Ensure all documents are ready before the deadline.

  • Step 3: Submit Your Application

    Complete our online application form and submit all required documents. Our admissions team will review your application holistically.

  • Step 4: Receive Your Decision

    Admission decisions are sent out in early May. We look forward to welcoming the next generation of masters to Juniper City.
